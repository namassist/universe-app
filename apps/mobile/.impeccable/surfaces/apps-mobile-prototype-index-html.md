# Surface brief: Universe mobile app (prototype)

Scope: the mobile client for three roles, as a clickable HTML prototype at
phone size that stage 3 (Compose Multiplatform) translates screen for screen.
Mode: Operate.

Audience and job:

- Operator (role User): before the shift, at the mess, often before dawn: am I
  a holder or a spare today, which unit, why, and did my FTW pass. Afterwards:
  history. The phone is handed in during work.
- Pengawas Lapangan: in the pit, often in full sun: which units are empty,
  who sits where, unit status, scan an ID card. Read only.
- Superadmin: the board from anywhere: place by hand, watch the timeline run,
  reset a password or deactivate an account.

Constraints: Bahasa Indonesia; follows the phone's light or dark theme; data
shown is synthetic and labeled so; never reveal a spare's seat before
finger-second (docs/domain-map.md, Tickets).

Unresolved: Laporan and Dashboard detail screens; iOS specifics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern everyday app, the category standard played straight at the
craft level of the owner's reference wallet app. It refuses metaphor: no pit,
slip or signage costume; the unit code and its status carry the screen.

OWN-WORLD: Universe navy hero band with the cyan brand mark; below it a
rounded sheet on a neutral ground, one corner language (20px sheets, 14px
tiles, full pills). One action colour per theme (blue in light, cyan in
dark); status in green, amber, red, always with a word. Figtree, tabular
numerals for codes and times.

STORY: The operator opens the app and knows in two seconds: unit, holder or
spare, why, and what is still owed (FTW, finger) before the bus.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy hero, top: wordmark, shift chip, help. Left: unit code
at display size, status line under it, formation and area link. Right: two
stacked actions, Kartu ID and Tiket. Under: status pills. Then the sheet with
a 4-column menu grid. Bottom bar of five, raised centre button.

FORM: canon (category standard), chosen by the owner after two re-rolls,
"too conceptual"; seed key 569c315c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
